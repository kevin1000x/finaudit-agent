```text
ar_turnover_days:        365 * bs.accounts_receivable / is.operating_revenue_current
interest_bearing_ratio:  (bs.short_term_borrowings + bs.trading_financial_liabilities
                          + bs.non_current_liabilities_due_within_one_year
                          + bs.long_term_borrowings + bs.bonds_payable
                          + bs.lease_liabilities) / bs.total_assets
```
